# SimpleBrush

A lightweight mod that unlocks natural resource brushes in-game and lets you restore depleted resources with one click.

## Features

**Resource Brush Unlock**

- Reveals hidden Ore, Oil, Fertile Land, and Ground Water brushes in the Terraforming toolbar
- Works in both Game and Editor modes

**Restore Depleted Resources**

- One-click buttons in Mod Settings to reset used resources back to full
- Individual buttons for Fertility, Ore, Oil, and Fish
- "Restore All" button to reset everything at once
- Confirmation dialogs to prevent accidental clicks

**Extraction Stability**

- Optimizes replenishment cadence in unlimited resource mode to prevent extraction buildings from stalling or dropping to zero efficiency between refill cycles.

## Compatibility

- **ExtraLandscapingTools (ELT)**: Overlapping features, no conflicts, and the two can be installed together. Both re-parent the same vanilla brush prefabs, so no duplicate toolbar entries appear, but the brush icons depend on mod load order.
- **Self-contained**: nothing else to install.

## Installation

Subscribe on [Paradox Mods](https://mods.paradoxplaza.com/) or place the DLL in your local Mods folder.

## Acknowledgements

Thanks to Triton Supreme, author of ExtraLandscapingTools and ExtraLib, for being first to surface these hidden vanilla brushes. Reading that work confirmed the approach was viable. SimpleBrush is an independent implementation.

# SimpleBrush 自然资源笔刷与资源恢复

解锁游戏内置的自然资源笔刷，并支持在设置中恢复已耗尽的自然资源。

## 功能

**自然资源笔刷解锁**

- 在地形改造工具栏中显示游戏内置的矿石、石油、肥沃土地与地下水笔刷
- 支持常规游戏模式与地图编辑器模式

**恢复已耗尽资源**

- 在模组设置面板中一键将已使用的资源量恢复至满额
- 提供肥沃度、矿石、石油与鱼类资源的单独恢复按钮，以及“全部恢复”选项
- 包含确认提示对话框，防止误触

**持续抽取稳定性**

- 优化无限资源模式下的补充节拍，避免抽取类建筑因两轮补充间隔过长而出现效率归零停产

## 兼容性

- **ExtraLandscapingTools（ELT）**：功能有重叠但互不冲突，可同时安装。两者改写的是同一批原版笔刷 prefab，工具栏不会出现重复条目；但笔刷图标取决于模组加载顺序。
- **自包含**：无需安装其他前置。

## 安装

在 Paradox Mods 订阅，或将编译后的 DLL 放入本地 Mods 目录。

## 致谢

感谢 ExtraLandscapingTools 与 ExtraLib 的作者 Triton Supreme 最早在游戏中暴露出这些隐藏的原版笔刷，本模组的可行性判断参考了这项工作。SimpleBrush 为独立实现。
